    🧠 How the score works

    Low light (≈30% weight)

    Jacks are ambush predators that hunt hardest at dawn, dusk and through the night. Civil/nautical twilight scores highest, night is strong, bright midday is weakest — though heavy cloud cover lifts daytime scores. A run-in start that lands in low light (dawn, dusk or night) gets an extra bonus — that combination scores highest of all.

    Tide (≈40%) — run-in start is the top driver

    Start of the incoming tide scores highest: from the low-tide turn through the first ~2 h of the run-in, peaking ~30–90 min after the turn, when water starts pushing back into drains, snags and mangrove edges. Next best: top of the run-in and the high turn / first of the run-out, then the last of the run-out (bait flushed from drains). Mid run-out and dead slack score lowest. Bigger-range (spring) tides boost the run-in modestly.

    Run-in start is timed for your location: on Baffle Creek from the along-creek model's low turn (including any benchmark corrections you applied); at Essendean / the upper creek, where lows are a truncated pool level, it uses the time the water starts rising (calibrated from the Essendean gauge: ≈ 10.2 − 2.48 × Bundaberg high-tide height (LAT) hours after the Bundaberg high, i.e. ~2.3–4.4 h; earlier on bigger tides), and is skipped on neap tides that don't reach. Elsewhere it uses the Open-Meteo offshore model + your tide offset. The window is widened by the local timing uncertainty (e.g. ±30 min at the coast, ±45–150 min upstream), so uncertain spots get a broader, flatter bump rather than a falsely precise one.

    Solunar (≈16%)

    Major periods: ~2 hours centred on the moon overhead (upper transit) and underfoot (lower transit). Minor periods: ~1 hour centred on moonrise and moonset. Scores taper toward the edges.

    Moon phase (≈5%)

    New and full moons (bigger tides, stronger feeding cues) get a boost; quarter moons are neutral.

    Weather (≈9%)

    Falling barometer and the build-up to storms boost the score; warm humid conditions help; a rapidly rising barometer, strong wind (>25 km/h) and heavy rain are light penalties.

    Water temperature (multiplier)

    Sea-surface temperature scales the whole score: strong above ~24°C, fair 22–24°C, sluggish below ~20°C. If SST is unavailable a seasonal estimate is used (peak Oct–Apr in QLD). Note: estuary water is often warmer than the offshore SST shown.

    📏 Queensland rules reminder

    Mangrove jack in Queensland tidal waters: minimum size 35 cm, possession limit 5 (per Fisheries Queensland, page last updated 25 Sep 2026). Rules change — always check the current limits before you keep a fish: Size and possession limits in tidal waters (qld.gov.au). Also check closed waters, green zones and seasonal closures for other species (e.g. the Oct/Nov coral reef fin fish closures) via the Fisheries Queensland rules or the free Qld Fishing 2.0 app.

    🧪 How predictions are made

    1 · Base tide: Bundaberg (Burnett Heads) harmonic model

    41 tidal constituents (M2, S2, N2, K2, K1, O1, P1, Q1, M4, MS4, MN4, M6 …, with nodal corrections) fitted by least squares to the MSQ Bundaberg port gauge 051011A observed 10-minute record 2021–2025 (≈260k readings). Runs offline for any date. Validation: vs official MSQ 2026 high/low predictions (1411 tides) HW timing RMS 4.5 min, LW 4.9 min, height RMS 0.02 m; vs held-out observed gauge data 2–9 Oct 2026, height RMS 0.06 m, HW timing RMS 12 min. Official MSQ 2026 Bundaberg times are also embedded and shown for comparison.

    2 · Along-creek transfer (calibration nodes)

    Each Bundaberg high/low is shifted in time and mapped in height using the values at your spot, interpolated linearly by distance between nodes; the curve between turns uses the standard cosine interpolation. Heights are m AHD (Bundaberg LAT→AHD −1.693 m per TMR; Winfield AHD ≈ LAT −1.53 assumes local MSL≈AHD, unverified).

    3 · What the Essendean gauge shows

    BoM 039267 (gauge zero = 0.00 m AHD, event-reported in 5 cm steps). 2–9 Oct 2026: 10 tidal peaks, on average 5 h 39 min (sd 44 min, range 4:44–6:51) after the observed Bundaberg high, i.e. ≈ 4 h 34 min after the MSQ-predicted Winfield high. Peak level ≈ −0.52 + 0.42 × Bundaberg HW (m LAT), r = 0.93. Lows are truncated at 0.37–0.57 m AHD (bed/fresh water pool), so the creek there drains for ~7 h and floods in 1–2 h. Bundaberg highs ≤ 2.13 m LAT produced no visible peak; ≥ 2.35 m did — Essendean only “sees” the bigger tide of each day in this period.

    4 · Limitations
    • Upstream calibration is one week (2–9 Oct 2026), low steady fresh flow (Mimdale 0.78 m all week). Floods or heavy rain will delay/flatten tides and raise levels upstream — expect big errors after rain.
    • No calibration between Winfield (3.4 km) and the Hills Rd gauge, which is on Euleilah Creek (≈25 km channel distance) and used as a proxy for the 16–35 km reach.
    • Mouth timing is assumed equal to Bundaberg/Bargara (open coast, MSQ planes); the Winfield offsets are MSQ “non-linear transfer” values (**), not a long gauge record.
    • Above Essendean the tidal limit is unknown — values are held at Essendean’s and flagged.
    • Wind, pressure and swell can shift real levels by 0.1–0.3 m (use the live residual).
    ⚠️ Not for navigation. Predictions are estimates. Always use the official MSQ tide tables and local knowledge; Baffle Creek’s bar and shallows change.

    🔒 Sharing

    Privacy — what is shared?
    Your log (incl. exact GPS, spot names, notes and bait details) is stored only on this phone. If you turn sharing on, each saved session is sent with only: the ~2 km creek stretch (e.g. “km 4–6, Winfield–Flat Rock”) or, off Baffle Creek, a broad area (e.g. “Burnett River / Bundaberg”); times rounded to 15-minute slots; species, counts, kept/released, longest length; bait type; the app’s predicted score / tide / light / solunar / moon / wind values; and a random anonymous id (no name, account, device id or GPS). Submissions go through a public no-account relay (ntfy.sh) to the app’s maintainer box, which validates them and publishes the combined table as data/catches.csv (public). Deleting a shared session here also removes it from the shared table at the next update.
