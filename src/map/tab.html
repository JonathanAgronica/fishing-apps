Long-press the map near the creek (or tap ➕ Add spot, then tap) to drop your own named spot — it snaps to the creek centreline for tide timing but keeps the exact point you tapped. Drag your pins to move them. Give each spot a type — Run-in (RI, yellow), Top of tide (TOT, green), Bottom of tide (BOT, purple) or Run-out (RO, pink) — and its bite score favours that stage of its own tide. Tap any spot for its next high/low/run-in start and bite score. Your spots appear in Bite times, Tides and the Catch log too.
