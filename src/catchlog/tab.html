🔒 Sharing

Privacy — what is shared?
Your log (incl. exact GPS, spot names, notes and bait details) and your own map spots & renamed landmarks are stored only on this phone. If you turn sharing on, each saved session is sent with only: the river system and ~2 km stretch (e.g. “km 4–6, Winfield–Flat Rock” on Baffle Creek, “km 0–2, Burnett River”) or, off the mapped channels, a broad area (e.g. “Off-creek: near Noosa River”); times rounded to 15-minute slots; species, counts, kept/released, longest length; bait type; the app’s predicted score / tide / light / solunar / moon / wind values; and a random anonymous id (no name, account, device id or GPS). Submissions go through a public no-account relay (ntfy.sh) to the app’s maintainer box, which validates them and publishes the combined table as data/catches.csv (public). Deleting a shared session here also removes it from the shared table at the next update.

Import old catches (any year back to 1990)A plain CSV with a header row works, e.g.
date,time,spot,km,caught,count,size,species,hours,notes
2014-03-08,6pm,Flat Rock Rd ramp,,y,2,48cm,jack,3,run-in
12/01/2009,dawn,,22.5,n,,,,2,
Only date is required. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY. Times can be rough (18:30, 6pm, dawn, dusk, morning, arvo, night; blank = 7am). Spot = a landmark or My-spot name, or give km (plus line for side creeks, e.g. mosq / Mosquito, with km from its junction). caught y/n; size in cm (mm/inch ok); optional colour = lure colour; hours defaults to 2; a row with a catch but no hours becomes a catch-only 30-min entry centred on the time (presence-only in the stats). Tide, moon, solunar & sun are computed from the model; weather is marked unknown for old dates. Re-importing the same file skips duplicates.
