How the traffic light works

Dimmed rows are outside your session hours or in the dark. Wind in knots (direction it blows from); waves are the nearest offshore model cell. Tap a row for the reasons.

Bite score by hour

Colour = bite score; a dark outline means the traffic light is amber or red for that hour.
